About

Cooking the north of India, slowly

Saffron & Salt opened its doors with a simple idea: cook North Indian food the long way. Our tandoor is lit early each morning for breads and skewers, our gravies are built from slow-simmered tomato and onion masalas, and the dal is left on a low flame for the better part of the day. Nothing arrives frozen and nothing is rushed.

The dining room is warm, unhurried and made for long tables — families sharing a thali, friends working through a plate of kebabs, or a quiet lunch over biryani and chai. Our team is happy to talk you through spice levels, suggest a wine or lassi pairing, and box up whatever you cannot finish.
